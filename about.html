Our Story

North Star Bakery began with a simple goal: to create fresh, handmade baked goods that bring neighbors together. Our bakery is a welcoming place for families, commuters, local customers, and event planners.

Our Sourcing Philosophy

We believe good baking starts with good ingredients. We carefully select quality flour, seasonal ingredients, and locally sourced products whenever possible. Our goal is to support nearby suppliers while creating delicious baked goods for our community.

Meet Our Team

Our bakers and staff share a passion for handmade food and friendly service. From early morning baking to helping customers choose the perfect celebration cake, our team works together to make every visit welcoming.
